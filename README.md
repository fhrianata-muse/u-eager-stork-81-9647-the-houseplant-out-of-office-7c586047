One line, 114 characters — the funniest out-of-office auto-reply a houseplant would send:

> Out of office: my roots have grown through the keyboard. Your email is now fertiliser. I will reply when I flower.

`main.py` keeps the line as data and writes it to `/output/entry.txt` only after the 1–120 character, single-line gate checks out at run time.
